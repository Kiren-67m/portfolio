Loan Default Prediction

Mar 2026

  • Joined and cleaned three relational tables (70K borrowers, 180K loans, 627K payment records) in Python, handling missing values, duplicates, orphaned foreign keys, and inconsistent formatting to produce a single loan-level modeling dataset.
  • Engineered payment behavior features from monthly repayment records including on-time rate, delinquency rate, and cumulative delinquent days. In exploratory analysis, payment behavior showed stronger associations with default than credit score or income.
  • Built models for default classification and days-to-default regression, achieving an AUC of 0.96 and an R² of 0.79 in the competition evaluation. Delivered a 12-slide visual report with risk monitoring recommendations.
